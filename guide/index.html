Before you start

Hi, I'm Timothy.

I build AI systems for a living, and I run Spero Automations. We set up automations for ID firms so enquiries get answered and followed up.

Most firm owners I talk to tried ChatGPT once, got a generic answer, and stopped. The trick is simple: tell it your real situation, and tell it exactly what you want back. That's what the five prompts in here do.

How to use them

Open ChatGPT. The free app is enough. Copy a prompt, swap the [blue parts in brackets] for your own details, and send.

Three rules before you paste anything

  1. Leave out personal details.No NRIC numbers, full addresses, phone numbers or bank details. A first name is enough. Under the PDPA your clients' data is your firm's responsibility.
  2. Stop ChatGPT training on your chats.In ChatGPT, open Settings → Data controls and switch off Improve the model for everyone. Skip the thumbs up and down under answers too: rating a reply can still share that chat with OpenAI. For anything sensitive, start a Temporary chat.
  3. Read it before you send it.ChatGPT writes fast and sounds sure, but it can get numbers and facts wrong. You're still the one sending it.

01 · Enquiries

Turn a messy enquiry into a clean lead summary.

For when a WhatsApp or Qanvast message comes in and it's all over the place.

The prompt
You help an interior design firm in Singapore sort new enquiries. From the message below, pull out:
- Name
- Property (HDB, condo or landed; BTO or resale; how many rooms)
- Key collection or move-in date
- Budget
- Works they mentioned
- Anything they're worried about
Write "not mentioned" for anything missing.
Then suggest the 3 questions I should ask next, in a friendly WhatsApp tone.

Message:
[paste the enquiry here]
They send

hi saw ur work on qanvast. collecting keys for 4rm bto in tengah around feb. budget maybe 45k? want kitchen and both toilets redone plus some carpentry. wife likes japandi. how long u all take ah

You get back (shortened)
Property
4-room BTO, Tengah
Keys
Around February
Budget
About S$45,000
Works
Kitchen, two bathrooms, carpentry. Style: Japandi
Worried about
How long it takes
  1. Congrats on the keys! Can I get your name, and is February confirmed?
  2. How much carpentry are you thinking, like full-height wardrobes or a TV console?
  3. Do you have the floor plan? Once I see it I can give you a rough timeline.

Tip: keep this chat open and paste each new enquiry into it. ChatGPT remembers the instructions, so after the first one you only paste the message.

02 · Follow-ups

Follow up a homeowner who went quiet.

For when the first meeting went well, and then nothing. Quiet usually means busy, not no.

The prompt
Write 3 short WhatsApp follow-ups to a homeowner who went quiet after our first meeting.

What we discussed: [e.g. 4-room resale in Punggol, they liked the 3D for the living room, waiting for their bank loan]
How long ago: [e.g. 2 weeks]

Rules: under 50 words each. Friendly, no pressure, no discounts. Sound like a person, not a company. One of them should offer something useful, like a rough timeline or a material sample.
One of the three you get back

Hi Rachel, hope the loan stuff is going smoothly! No rush on our side. If it helps, I can send you a rough timeline for the living room so you can plan the move. Just let me know.

Tip: pick the one that sounds most like you and change a few words, so it reads like you wrote it. Then set yourself a reminder to try once more a week later.

03 · Site visits

Turn site-visit notes into a recap and a to-do list.

For when you've just walked a unit and everything is still in your head.

The prompt
Below are my rough notes from a site visit. Turn them into two things:
1. A short WhatsApp recap for the client: what we saw, what we agreed, what happens next.
2. A to-do list for my team, with who does what if I said so.
Don't add anything I didn't say.

Notes:
[talk or paste your notes here]
Your notes

ok 5rm resale. kitchen wall can hack confirmed so island ok. toilet floor uneven need screeding. aircon trunking in living room very ugly client wants to hide. send revised layout by fri. ah huat check if can shift db box

You get back (shortened)

For the client: Thanks for having us today! Quick recap: the kitchen wall can be hacked, so the island works. The bathroom floor needs levelling first. We'll look at ways to hide the aircon trunking in the living room. I'll send the revised layout by Friday.

  • Revised layout with kitchen island: you, by Friday
  • Check if the DB box can be moved: Ah Huat
  • Price the bathroom floor screeding
  • Options to hide the living room aircon trunking

Tip: you don't have to type. Tap the microphone in the ChatGPT app and just talk through the visit before you drive off, while it's fresh.

04 · Quotations

Explain a quotation in plain English, and in Chinese.

For first-time renovators who nod along but don't really know what "screeding" is. And for their parents, who are often paying.

The prompt
Here are some lines from a renovation quotation. Explain each line in plain English for a homeowner who has never renovated before. One or two sentences per line.
Don't change any numbers and don't add items.
Then write the same explanation in simple Chinese for their parents.

[paste the quotation lines, without the client's name or address]
The quote says

Hacking of existing kitchen wall (approx. 3m) incl. disposal: $1,200

You get back

We'll knock down the kitchen wall (about 3 metres long) and take all the debris away. $1,200.

我们会拆掉厨房的墙（约3米长），并把所有废料清走。费用是1,200元。

Tip: check every number against your original quote, and if you don't read Chinese yourself, ask someone who does to glance at it before it goes out.

05 · WhatsApp

Write replies for the questions you get every day.

For "how much for a 4-room?" at 11pm. Write the answers once, then send them in two taps.

The prompt
I run an interior design firm in Singapore. Write short WhatsApp replies to these questions homeowners ask us all the time. Friendly, under 60 words each, and end each one with a question that keeps the chat going.
1. How much for a 4-room BTO?
2. When can you start?
3. Can I see your past projects?
4. Can you come down to see the unit?
5. [a question you get a lot]
For the price question, don't give a number. Say what it depends on and offer a free consult.
One you get back

Hi! It really depends on what you want done, like how much carpentry and whether you're redoing the kitchen or bathrooms. Most of our 4-room BTO clients start with a free consult where we go through the floor plan together. When do you collect your keys?

Then save them as quick replies in the WhatsApp Business app: open the Tools tab, then Quick replies, and add one with a shortcut like price. In any chat, type / and pick it. You can keep up to 50.
