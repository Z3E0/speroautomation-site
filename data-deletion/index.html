Data deletion

Delete your data with one email.

How to have Spero delete the personal data it holds about you, and how a business disconnects its WhatsApp from Spero.

Send a deletion request

Last updated 19 November 2026

How to ask

  1. Email hello@speroautomation.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us the phone number or email address you used with us. If you messaged a business we work for, tell us its name too.
  3. We may ask you to confirm from that number or address, so we never delete someone else's data by mistake.

To: hello@speroautomation.com Subject: Delete my data Message: Please delete my data. I used +65 9123 4567.

What we delete

Within 30 days of confirming your request, we delete:

  • your WhatsApp messages, our replies and anything our assistant remembers about you;
  • the lead details and bookings we hold about you;
  • messages you sent through our website, and your place on our email lists;
  • copies of your messages in our internal alerts.

Copies in backups are deleted when those backups are deleted, and are not used for anything in the meantime. We email you when it is done.

We keep a record that you asked. If you asked us to stop messaging you, we also keep your number on a do-not-contact note, so we never message you again.

If you messaged a business we work for

That business keeps its own lead log in its own Google Sheet and is responsible for it. We delete what we hold, then pass your request to the business so it can delete its records. You can also ask the business directly.

If you connected through Facebook

If you used Facebook to connect a WhatsApp Business account to Spero, you can remove our app yourself: in Facebook, open Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations, find Spero Automations and choose Remove.

Removing the app stops any new data reaching us. To delete what we already hold, send us the email above as well.

If your business wants to disconnect

Email us and we disconnect our system from your WhatsApp Business account. Within 30 days we delete your access tokens and the conversations and data we hold for you. Your WhatsApp Business account, your number and your Google Sheet stay yours, and nothing in them is deleted unless you ask.

You can also remove Spero's access yourself in your Meta business settings at any time.
