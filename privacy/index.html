Who we are

Spero Automations ("Spero", "we", "us") is a business of BitBridge Labs Private Limited, based in Singapore. We set up AI automations for businesses, mainly interior design firms. Our main service is an AI assistant on a firm's WhatsApp number that answers new enquiries, asks the questions the firm's designers need, books consultations and hands each lead to a designer.

This policy covers our website, speroautomation.com, our own WhatsApp number, +65 8337 4686, and the service we run for the businesses we work for.

Whose data, and who decides

When you deal with Spero itself, by using our website, emailing us or messaging our WhatsApp number, Spero decides how your data is used and is responsible for it.

When you message a business we work for, that business is responsible for your data. We handle it only to provide our service to that business and on its instructions, as its data intermediary under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). The business's own privacy policy applies as well, and requests about that data are best sent to the business first. If you send them to us, we pass them on.

What we collect

From our website

Our website has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. When you fill in one of its forms, we receive what you type: your name and email address and, depending on the form, your company, your message, the channel you would like us to test and the figures you set in the lead leak calculator.

As with any website, the services that deliver the page to you receive your IP address and browser details. For us those are GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Fonts, which supplies its typefaces.

When you message Spero on WhatsApp

Your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, the messages you send and the replies we send. If you send a photo, file, voice note or location, we record that you sent one and keep any caption. Our assistant does not open them.

When a business connects its WhatsApp to Spero

A business we work for connects its WhatsApp Business account to our system, either through Meta's sign-up flow or by giving us access in its own Meta business account. Meta then gives us:

  • the IDs of the business, its WhatsApp Business account and its phone numbers;
  • an access token that lets our system send and receive WhatsApp messages for the business;
  • the business's message templates;
  • the messages sent to and from its WhatsApp number.

We also get access to the consultation calendar and the Google Sheet that the business chooses to share with us, to book consultations and to keep its lead log.

When you message a business we work for

If you message a firm that uses Spero, our system handles your phone number and WhatsApp profile name, your messages and the replies, the answers you give about your project (such as your home type, whether it is new or resale, when you get your keys and your rough budget), and any consultation you book, move or cancel.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, and to arrange a call or a consultation.
  • For a business we work for: to answer its enquiries, ask the questions its designers need, book, move or cancel consultations in its calendar, follow up when someone goes quiet (at most three times), alert its staff, and keep its lead log in its own Google Sheet.
  • To send what you asked for, such as our free guide or the monthly Keys Coming calendar.
  • To keep the service working and safe: fixing errors, stopping misuse and checking that the assistant follows the rules it is given.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. We never use one business's customer data for another business.

Replies written by AI

Messages to our WhatsApp number, and to the numbers of businesses we work for, may be answered by an AI assistant built on Claude, a model made by Anthropic. It reads the conversation to write each reply and works within rules set by the business, such as what it may answer and which consultation times are open.

It says it is an AI assistant if you ask. When it cannot help, or when you ask for a person, it hands the chat to a person, who may read the conversation and reply instead.

Who else sees it

We run our automation software on servers we operate ourselves. We use these service providers, each only for the job listed:

Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)
Delivers WhatsApp messages.
Anthropic
Writes the assistant's replies. Its commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on this data.
Google
Holds a business's lead log (Google Sheets) and consultation calendar (Google Calendar), both owned by that business.
Telegram
Carries our internal alerts about conversations that need a person, with a copy of the messages.
Web3Forms
Delivers messages from our website's forms to our email.
Namecheap
Hosts our email.
GitHub
Hosts our website.

A business we work for sees the conversations and lead details of its own customers. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Data handled outside Singapore

Some of these providers store or process data outside Singapore, including in the United States. When data leaves Singapore, we rely on providers whose terms require them to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

How long we keep it

  • WhatsApp conversations held by our system are deleted automatically after 30 days. If you have asked us to stop messaging you, or the assistant has been switched off for your number, we keep a note of that so we go on respecting it.
  • A business's lead log is kept in that business's own Google Sheet, and the business decides how long to keep it.
  • Website form messages stay in our email for as long as we need to reply and follow up, or until you ask us to delete them. You stay on the Keys Coming list until you unsubscribe.
  • A business's access tokens and data are kept while we provide our service. Within 30 days of the business ending the service or disconnecting us, we delete its access tokens and the data we hold for it.
  • Backups: data in a backup is deleted when that backup is deleted, and is not used for anything else in the meantime.

How we protect it

Access tokens and keys are stored encrypted. Our automation server is not open to the internet: only the addresses that receive incoming messages can be reached, through a relay. Only people at Spero who need to see conversations can see them, and we keep our software up to date with security fixes.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify everyone the law requires us to, including the business concerned and the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Your choices

  • Stop the assistant. On WhatsApp, ask it to stop messaging you, or ask for a person. It stops replying and hands the chat to a person.
  • Unsubscribe from our emails by replying "unsubscribe".
  • See or correct your data. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.
  • Withdraw your consent to us using your data. We will tell you what that means for any service you have asked for.
  • Delete your data. See Delete your data.

If your request is about data a business we work for is responsible for, we pass it to that business and help it act on it.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects the businesses we work for, we tell them directly.
